ABOUT JHUY

A growing group built around real market needs.

JHUY began with businesses serving two very different but essential markets: transportation and fresh produce. Today, JHUY Holdings provides a common platform for those operating companies and for future ventures.

Our approach is practical: build dependable operations, work with strong commercial partners, serve customers well and grow responsibly over time.
